SUS Scoring Methodology

The System Usability Scale (SUS) computes a percentile usability score based on standard ISO 9241-11 criteria:

SUS Score = [Σ(Qodd - 1) + Σ(5 - Qeven)] × 2.5
  • Scores > 80.3: Grade A (Excellent - SaaS Standard)
  • Scores 68 - 80: Grade B (Good Acceptability)
  • Scores < 51: Grade F (Requires Re-engineering)
